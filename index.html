Computational biology

A reproducible Snakemake workflow for predicted cancer neoepitope candidates arising from alternative splicing. It is a modern reimplementation of the 2015 computational proof of concept.

Predicted candidates only — no experimental validation, clinical use, therapeutic claim, publication, or patent claim.
